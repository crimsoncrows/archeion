Your Privacy

Your uploaded file and pasted text are never saved or added to the repository. They're only used to run the comparison. Your similarity results can be viewed by professors, but your submitted file cannot.

Tips for Better Results

Submit your full manuscript, or at least your abstract and methodology, for the most accurate score. Very short inputs give less reliable results. Scanned PDFs without selectable text can't be read, so use a text-based PDF, DOCX, or TXT file.

What the Score Means

A high score doesn't mean plagiarism. It means your study covers similar ground as existing research. Use the matched studies to check your topic's originality and to find related work you should cite.

What You're Compared Against

Your input is compared only with the academic studies stored in Archeion. It doesn't search the internet or outside databases, so a low score here doesn't guarantee your study is unique elsewhere.

Natural Language Processing

Archeion analyzes the meaning of your text against stored studies and generates a comparison matrix highlighting relevant similarities. Results are produced automatically and should be reviewed by you and your adviser.

How It Works

Here's what happens after you submit your input.

  1. 1

    Submit your input

    Upload a file or paste your text. Word limits and accepted formats are shown on each tab.

  2. 2

    Text is converted to embeddings

    Your content is turned into vectors that capture its meaning, not just matching words.

  3. 3

    Compared against the database

    Your vectors are compared with the academic studies stored in Archeion to find the closest matches.

  4. 4

    Get your report

    You'll see an overall similarity score, the matched studies, and can download the report.
