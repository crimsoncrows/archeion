UNDERSTANDING YOUR RESULTS

The score shows how close your input is in meaning to studies in the repository, not how much text was copied. Archeion converts both texts into vector embeddings and compares them, so studies can match even when they use different words. Only studies scoring {{ threshold_pct }}% or higher are listed. {{ threshold_pct }}% to {{ high_pct|add:'-1' }}% is Moderate, meaning you should review and cite the match. {{ high_pct }}% and above is High, meaning your topic strongly overlaps with existing work. A high score is not plagiarism. It means a similar study already exists, so open it, see what it covers, and consider narrowing your scope or adding something it lacks. Results only cover studies stored in Archeion, and scanned PDFs may extract poorly, so pasting the text directly can give a better result.

The comparison matrix places your input side by side with the matched studies, showing where they overlap and where your work differs. It is generated by AI once per check from your text and the matched studies' records, so use it as a starting point for your review, not a final judgment. Always confirm its points against the actual studies. If there are no matches or the matrix could not be generated, only the similarity scores are shown.
