By accessing and using the Archeion Research Repository, you agree to be bound by the following terms and conditions. These terms apply to all users of the system, including students, faculty, and staff of Tarlac State University.

1. Account Management

Archeion is built for the TSU academic community. Access is available to registered TSU students, faculty, and staff. Please use your own account credentials; sharing or borrowing someone else's login isn't permitted, as it helps us keep everyone's research and data secure. You are responsible for keeping your password confidential and for keeping the email address on your account current, since password resets are sent to that address.

2. Academic Integrity

Archeion employs content similarity detection technology to uphold academic integrity. Any attempt to manipulate, misrepresent, or circumvent this system constitutes a violation of TSU's academic policies and may be subject to disciplinary action.

3. Similarity Checks

Files and text submitted for a similarity check are processed only to generate the result and are not saved to the repository. Students' similarity results are visible only to the student and to their professors; professors can view the results but not the submitted file itself. Similarity scores and the generated feature comparison are automated, advisory aids — they do not by themselves establish plagiarism or originality, and final academic judgment rests with faculty.

4. Acceptable Use

Users agree to use the platform solely for legitimate academic research and reference purposes. Reproduction, redistribution, or commercial use of any content within the repository without proper attribution and authorization is prohibited. Uploads are limited to 10 MB per file, and automated, excessive, or abusive requests may be rate-limited or blocked.

5. System Access

The Archeion development team and college administration reserve the right to suspend or revoke access to any user found to be in violation of these terms or engaging in conduct detrimental to the integrity of the repository.

6. Modifications

These terms may be updated periodically. Continued use of the system following any changes constitutes acceptance of the revised terms. Users are encouraged to review this page regularly.

7. Data & Privacy

Archeion collects only the information necessary for account management and repository operations, along with any feedback you choose to submit. To operate, Archeion relies on third-party service providers for hosting, database, file storage, email delivery, and AI-assisted analysis; text submitted for a similarity check may be sent to an AI service solely to generate the feature comparison. Archeion does not sell user data or share it for any other purpose. All data is handled in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and TSU's data privacy policies.
