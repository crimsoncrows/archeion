Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

Archeion is committed to protecting the privacy and personal information of all users within the Tarlac State University academic community. This policy outlines how user data is collected, used, and safeguarded within the system, in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

1. Information We Collect

Upon registration, Archeion collects your full name, institutional email address, and password, which is stored in encrypted (hashed) form and is never visible to anyone, including administrators. Archeion also keeps any profile updates you make and any feedback ratings you choose to submit on similarity results. No additional personal data is required.

2. How We Use Your Information

Collected information is used to authenticate user access, identify registered users within the system, send account emails such as password reset links, and improve the similarity check feature through user feedback. Users may browse the research repository, view trend analysis, and use the AI-powered similarity check feature.

3. Similarity Check Submissions

Files and text submitted for a similarity check are processed only to generate the result and are not saved to the repository. Students' similarity results are visible only to the student and to their professors; professors can view the results but not the submitted file itself.

4. Data Sharing

Archeion does not sell, trade, or share personal user information for marketing or any unrelated purpose. To operate, Archeion uses third-party service providers for web hosting, database, file storage, and email delivery, and an AI service that receives similarity check text solely to generate the feature comparison. Research documents in the repository are viewable only by verified TSU users for academic reference purposes.

5. Data Security

User data is stored with secured cloud service providers. Archeion uses encrypted connections, hashed passwords, role-based access control, and rate limiting to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of personal information.

6. Retention

User account data is retained for as long as the account remains active or as required by TSU's institutional records policies. Similarity check submissions are not retained after the result is generated.

7. Your Rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the right to access, correct, and request the removal of your personal information. You can update your profile and password directly in Archeion; for other requests, contact the College of Computer Studies.

8. Consent

By registering and using Archeion, users consent to the collection and use of their information as described in this policy.
